Privacy Policy for Clean Food Scanner

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Clean Food Scanner is designed to help users review food labels and ingredient information while keeping data collection limited and understandable.

Information Processed by the App

The app may process scanned label images or entered product details to provide food and ingredient information. Where possible, app preferences and recent choices are stored locally on your device.

Camera and Scanning

If you grant camera permission, the app uses the camera for scanning food labels or packaging. Camera access is used only for app functionality that you choose to start.

Diagnostics

If Apple provides crash reports or technical diagnostics to Zyverio, those reports may include device type, operating system version and technical information needed to improve reliability.

Data Sharing

Zyverio does not sell personal information from Clean Food Scanner. If the app relies on third-party services for product or ingredient lookup, those services may process technical request information needed to return results.

Your Choices

You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hornet.devlo@gmail.com.
