FAQ

What should I include in a support email?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Clean Food Scanner app version and a clear description of the issue.

Why does the app need camera access?

Camera access allows the app to scan food labels or packaging when you choose to use the scanning feature.

Can I request a product or ingredient feature?

Yes. Send focused feedback about the food information or scanning workflow you want improved.
