FAQ

Why does DriveWeather need location access?

Location access lets the app request weather information for the area relevant to your driving plans. You can also use manually entered places when supported.

Why can weather data differ from another app?

Forecasts can vary by provider, update timing, location precision and the way each app summarizes conditions.

What helps support investigate an issue?

Include your device model, iOS version, DriveWeather app version, approximate area searched and a description of the unexpected behavior.
