Privacy Policy for Focus Launcher

Effective date: July 23, 2026

This policy explains how Zyverio handles information in Focus Launcher. It is written to be clear and specific to the app rather than copied from a generic template.

Information We Collect

Focus Launcher is designed to function with minimal personal information. Shortcut names, configuration choices and app preferences are intended to remain on your device unless a system feature you choose, such as iCloud backup, stores them through Apple services.

Diagnostics

If Apple provides crash reports or performance diagnostics to Zyverio, those reports may include device type, operating system version and technical details needed to investigate reliability problems. Zyverio uses this information only to improve stability and does not use it to identify individual users.

Third Parties

Zyverio does not sell personal information from Focus Launcher. If the app links to external destinations you configure or open, those services are governed by their own privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Focus Launcher is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Zyverio, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Your Choices

You can remove app data by deleting Focus Launcher from your device. You can also manage Apple analytics sharing in iOS Settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hornet.devlo@gmail.com.
