FAQ

How do I report a problem?

Email hornet.devlo@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, Focus Launcher app version and the steps that reproduce the issue.

Does Focus Launcher store my shortcuts online?

The app is designed around local configuration. If you enable Apple backup or sync features, Apple may handle that storage according to your device settings.

Can I request a feature?

Yes. Zyverio welcomes focused feature requests that improve speed, clarity or privacy.
