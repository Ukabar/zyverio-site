FAQ

Why do results vary between tests?

Latency can change because of Wi-Fi quality, cellular signal, router load, VPNs, server distance and general internet congestion.

Is Ping Test a full speed test?

Ping Test focuses on responsiveness and latency. Download and upload throughput are different measurements.

What should I include in a support email?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Ping Test app version, connection type and a short description of the unexpected result.
