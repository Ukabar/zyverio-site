About Zyverio

Useful software, stripped to what matters.

Zyverio builds iOS applications with a simple philosophy: everyday tools should feel immediate, understandable and respectful. Each product is designed to do its job quickly while avoiding unnecessary data collection.

The current app family includes Focus Launcher, Ping Test and Clean Food Scanner, with more privacy-conscious utilities planned for the future.
