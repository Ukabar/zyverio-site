About

Software that keeps its promises small and useful.

Zyverio is an iOS software company focused on practical utilities with polished interfaces, strong performance and careful privacy choices.

Our Approach

We build apps around specific user needs, then refine the experience until the product feels simple without becoming shallow. Every feature has to earn its place.

Our Products

Focus Launcher, Ping Test and DriveWeather represent Zyverio's current direction: calm tools for attention, connectivity and mobility. Future apps will follow the same clarity-first standard.

Principles

What guides the work.

Clarity

Interfaces should make the next action obvious and reduce unnecessary decisions.

Performance

Lightweight software creates trust because it feels ready when the user is ready.

Privacy

Data collection should be limited, explained and connected to real product value.
