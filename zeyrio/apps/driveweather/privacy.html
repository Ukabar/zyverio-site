Privacy Policy for DriveWeather

Effective date: July 23, 2026

DriveWeather provides weather information for driving-related planning. This policy explains the data practices that support that purpose.

Location and Weather Data

DriveWeather may use a location you enter or permit through iOS to request weather information. Location data is used to provide relevant forecasts and condition summaries. Zyverio designs the app to avoid collecting location information beyond what is needed for weather functionality.

Local Preferences

The app may store preferences, recent searches or display settings on your device so the experience remains useful between sessions.

Weather Providers

Weather information may be requested from weather data providers. Those providers may receive technical request information necessary to return forecast data. Zyverio does not sell personal information from DriveWeather.

Diagnostics

If enabled by your iOS settings, Apple may share crash or performance diagnostics with Zyverio. These reports are used to improve reliability and are not used for advertising profiling.

Your Choices

You can manage location permission in iOS Settings, clear app data by deleting the app and control analytics sharing through Apple privacy settings.

Contact

Questions about DriveWeather privacy can be sent to privacy@zyverio.com.
