Privacy Policy for Ping Test

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Ping Test is built to provide network responsiveness information while keeping personal data collection limited.

Information Processed by the App

To perform a network test, the app may send technical requests to a server or endpoint needed for measurement. Results such as latency values and recent test state may be stored locally on your device for app functionality.

Personal Information

Zyverio does not require you to create an account to use Ping Test. The app is not designed to collect names, contacts, precise location or personal documents.

Diagnostics

Crash reports and technical diagnostics may be received through Apple developer services when users share analytics with developers. Zyverio reviews these reports to resolve defects and improve performance.

Data Sharing

Zyverio does not sell personal information from Ping Test. Network tests necessarily involve network communication, and the operators of networks or endpoints you use may process technical connection data under their own rules.

Your Choices

You can delete locally stored app data by removing the app from your device. You can manage analytics sharing through iOS Settings.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@zyverio.com.
